In 1870, in Bursa, a cook took a spit that had been horizontal for several thousand years and turned it vertical. This is the entire founding event. Everything after it is consequence.

The Bureau was not founded. The Bureau was ████████ and began taking notes.

Every fact on this page is true and independently verifiable. We have checked. We checked again. The name itself is a confession: shawarma descends from the Turkish çevirme, from çevirmek, meaning to turn. The food is not named for its meat, its bread, its spice, or its country. It is named after the rotation.

We did not choose this name. We inherited it.

EXHIBIT A · TOUM PROTOCOL

NO EGG.
NO DAIRY.
NO EXPLANATION.

Toum (ثوم) translates to "garlic." Not "garlic sauce." Just garlic. The sauce is so definitional that the language saw no reason to distinguish between the plant and the emulsion.

It contains garlic, oil, lemon, salt. Nothing else. No egg, no dairy, nothing that would normally hold an emulsion together. It is held up by garlic's own structure and the sheer will of whoever is making it.

THE EMULSION EVENT · CLASSIFIED. Add the oil too quickly and the emulsion collapses into oily separation. There is no partial credit. Approximately 1 in 4 first attempts survive. Survivors do not discuss it. The Bureau maintains no records of the failures, at the request of the families.

WAS NEVER
REAL.

Subject is not pink. Subject has never been pink. Those luminous magenta batons are turnips, lift (لفت), and turnips are white.

One slice of beetroot, introduced at brining, colours the entire vessel. The whole jar borrows its identity from a single unrelated vegetable sitting quietly in the corner.

The Bureau has questions about what else operates this way.
