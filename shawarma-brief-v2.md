## WHY THIS REWRITE EXISTS

A ruling was obtained from ansari.chat on using devotional language for a non-religious subject. Summary of the finding: applying sacred terminology, religious hierarchy, theological doctrine, or polytheistic concepts to a mundane subject — even playfully, even without malice — falls under *istihza' bid-din* (trivializing religion), and plural-"gods" or simulated-worship framing drifts toward shirk in language. Playful exaggeration about food is fully permissible; **borrowing sacred vocabulary to do it is not.**

That kills the "cult / Order of the Wrap" premise as written. It does **not** kill the site.

**The comedy was never the religion.** Re-read the addendum's own thesis: *"presenting verified fact in the register of a paranoid dossier."* The engine is the **classified-intelligence-file register** — the cult layer was a second coat of paint sitting on top of it. The site was running two competing metaphors (secret agency AND religious sect), which was already muddying the register. Removing one makes it *funnier*, not weaker: a single, ruthlessly consistent bureaucratic voice is a deeper comedy vein than a mixed one, because bureaucracy gives you forms, clearance tiers, redactions, case numbers, and compliance language — all of which are free jokes the cult frame couldn't reach.

**Every single fact in the lore addendum survives untouched.** Nothing true was lost. Only the vocabulary changed.

## THE ENGINE (restated)

Movie-trailer-grade production for a sandwich, played completely straight, in the voice of a government office that takes rotation extremely seriously. Every fact is real; the comedy is the *format*, never the food and never anyone's faith. Specificity is the joke engine — always prefer a date, a case number, or an untranslated Arabic term over any adjective.

The four original prohibitions stand: never resolve the origin question (Turkey/Syria/Lebanon/Palestine stays open forever), never translate Arabic inside jokes (glossary only), never name the film, never make the food the butt.

## BELOW THE FOLD

Order: STATEMENT OF FINDINGS → THE CELLS → STANDING DISPUTES → TOUM PROTOCOL / THE PICKLE STANDARD exhibits → GLOSSARY → THE HOURS → FIELD NOTE: THE COUNTER → CLEARANCE LEVELS → APPLY → footer.

- **THE CELLS** — world map, red string, five nodes (Bursa, Beirut, Puebla, Athens, Berlin). "THE BUREAU DOES NOT EXPAND. IT ARRIVES."
- **STANDING DISPUTES** — five case-file tabs, `STATUS: UNRESOLVED`. Keep casualty figures **unquantified**: invented numbers read as made-up and break the true-facts engine; vagueness reads as redacted. Use `CASUALTIES: UNRECORDED. FRIENDSHIPS: SEVERAL.`
- **GLOSSARY** — dictionary-straight, zero jokes inside the definitions (çevirme, toum, liyya, saj, lift, amba, kabees). The deadpan accuracy is the joke.
- **THE HOURS** — "Peak observed activity: 0200–0400 local. The Bureau does not keep business hours. The Bureau keeps the hours you keep when something has gone wrong or gone extremely well."
- **THE EMULSION EVENT** — "Approximately 1 in 4 first attempts survive. Survivors do not discuss it. The Bureau maintains no records of the failures, at the request of the families."
- **CLEARANCE LEVELS** (replaces tiers):
  - **CLEARANCE I — FOIL HANDLER.** Has said "no lettuce" without hesitation · identifies the shop by smell from outside · has never once asked what toum is
  - **CLEARANCE II — KEEPER OF THE EMULSION.** Has broken a toum and filed no report · knows the turnips are white · holds a position on fries-inside and will defend it
  - **CLEARANCE III — CHIEF INSPECTOR OF THE VERTICAL.** Has fought someone over the bill and won · knows why the tomato is up there · was present at a 3am counter with no queue and did not hesitate
- **APPLY = a clearance application, not a signup form** (the section's whole punchline). Header: `FORM 27-B — APPLICATION FOR ROTATIONAL CLEARANCE`
  - Radio, required: "Position on fries-inside" — FOR / AGAINST. No abstain option exists. Helper text: "There is no neutral party."
  - Checkbox: "I have broken a toum and filed no report."
  - Checkbox: "I know why the tomato is up there."
  - Submit label: **"APPROACH THE COUNTER"** · Success state: "YOU HAVE BEEN SEEN. DO NOT WAIT TO BE CALLED."
- **Footer, deadpan:** `DOCUMENT REF: SHW/1870/ROT-9 · CLASSIFICATION: OPEN · REVIEWED BY: NO ONE` — "The Bureau accepts no liability for emulsion failures. Serving suggestion is not a suggestion. Page 1 of 1. There is no page 2."
